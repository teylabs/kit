# DetailSection

One part of a record's detail page: the SectionHeader (`title`, `description`, `actions`, `level`) on the page ground and the body on a card of its own. A DescriptionList, List or EmptyState inside draws no second card.

Tailwind and the theme tokens only. Browser specimen: `?section-shapes` (`&dark`), with `KIT_SECTION_SHAPES_SCREENSHOT=<path prefix>` saving each scheme at 1440 and 390px.
